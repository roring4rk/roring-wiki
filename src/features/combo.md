# Combo system

> Vanilla pre-re: no kill-combo counter.
> Ours: every enemy kill adds to a combo counter and restarts a 60 s window; milestones grant
> +1% movement speed (capped at +24% at 2000 kills), shown bottom-right as damage-number digits.
> Why: rewards sustained farming with a small, visible movement bonus.
