# Maps & NPCs

Custom maps, NPCs, warps and changed spawns.

- **Population shells** (fake players): towns are populated by server-driven fake players
  (walk, chat, fight, vendorstalls, party with players). See
  [population-parity](https://github.com/roring4rk/ragnarok/blob/main/docs/notes/population-parity.md).
  > Vanilla pre-re: towns have NPCs only, no fake players.
  > Ours: population engine fills towns/fields with shells.
  > Why: a lived-in world.
  > Source: #19 / #169-#236.
